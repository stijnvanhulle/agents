- Title: `[Component or feature] - [What it does] [Context]`, such as
  `Button Component - Props, Variants, and Accessibility` or
  `Creating Plugins - Extend the Build Pipeline`.
- Description: `[Action verb] [feature]. [Primary benefit]. [Secondary benefit].` Specific, and
  about what the reader gets.

## Page structure

1. An H1, with a badge when one applies, then 2 to 3 sentences saying what it is, who it is for, and when to use it.
2. `## What is <topic>?`: a 2 to 3 sentence explanation.
3. `## Why use <topic>?`: what it saves the reader, with numbers where you have them.
4. `## How to use`: a minimal example with its explanation.
5. `## FAQ`, on a guide: 3 to 5 questions people actually search, such as "When should I use X or Y?" or
   "How do I <task>?" or "What's the difference between A and B?", each with a direct answer.
6. `## See also` and `## Next steps`: at least 2 or 3 internal links, to related features, a
   guide, the parent hub page, a tutorial, and the API reference.

## Checklist

- Title at most 60 characters, description at most 155.
- The intro answers what, who, and when.
- A FAQ with 3 to 5 questions on a guide.
- Descriptive headings, never "Overview".
- Paragraphs of at most 3 sentences.
